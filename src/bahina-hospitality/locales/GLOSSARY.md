# BAHINA Hospitality - Simple Language Glossary

This glossary guides all English and Marathi text in `src/bahina-hospitality`.
Our reader is a villager, a farmer's family, or a first-time traveller reading English at school level (up to 10th standard).

## Core Principles
1. **Short sentences**: Maximum 12 words per English sentence. Maximum 14 words per Marathi sentence. Average 9 words or less.
2. **Everyday words**: Use plain words people know. No jargon, no marketing fluff, no complicated grammar.
3. **Sentence case only**: Never use ALL-CAPS sentences, buttons, or chips.
4. **Action buttons**: 1 to 3 words starting with a verb (e.g. *See farmhouses*, *Ask to book*, *Call us*).
5. **Digits for numbers**: Always use digits (e.g. *46 km*, *12 guests*, *3 rooms*).

## Safety and Notice Rules
- **Rain notice**: "Rain closes the forest roads. Please ask the Forest Department before you go."
- **Wildlife notice**: "We cannot promise you will see animals. They live free in the wild forest."
- **Preview note**: "This is a design preview. No payment is taken."

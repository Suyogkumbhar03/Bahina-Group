## 1. Verified Facts Reference ("Checked" - Green)

The following claims are verified against official records and opened sources:

1. **Radhanagari Wildlife Sanctuary Area**: 351.16 sq km (notified 1958 as Dajipur Sanctuary, renamed Radhanagari in 1985).
2. **First Wildlife Sanctuary in Maharashtra**: Declared in 1958.
3. **Distance from Kolhapur City**: Approximately 46 km via state highway along the Bhogawati valley.
4. **Flagship Species**: Indian gaur (*Bos gaurus* / bison) is the main animal of Radhanagari forest.
5. **Bird Species**: 264 bird species recorded in sanctuary surveys.
6. **UNESCO World Heritage Inscription**: Western Ghats serial cluster inscribed in 2012 (Site ID 1342).
7. **Radhanagari Dam**: Built across the Bhogawati river.
8. **Dam Construction Timeline**: Initiated in 1907 by Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj; opened in 1954.
9. **7 Automatic Gates**: 7 gates open automatically by hydrostatic water pressure alone without electricity.
10. **Shivrai Sada Pond**: High-altitude plateau with a natural wildlife watering pond.
